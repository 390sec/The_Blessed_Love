## **How can you recognize genuine affection?**

I would look for these five things:

1\. **They choose you voluntarily.** They sometimes initiate conversations, spend time with you, or reach out without always needing something.

2\. **They are curious about you.** They want to know how you're feeling, what you're thinking, and what's happening in your life.

3\. **They make an effort.** Even if they're busy, shy, or emotionally reserved, they find their own ways of maintaining the connection.

4\. **They care about your feelings.** They may disagree with you or need space, but your happiness and pain are not irrelevant to them.

5\. **Their actions are reasonably consistent.** You don't have to keep guessing whether you matter to them based on a single good or bad interaction.

But remember: **these are signs of care, not definitive proof of romantic love.** Friendship, affection, gratitude, and romantic attraction can look similar.
