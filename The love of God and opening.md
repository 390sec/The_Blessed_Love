## **🤝 What is Trust?**

**Trust is the confidence that someone will treat you with honesty, care, respect, and reliability.**

Trust means:

- I believe your words because your actions support them.
- I feel safe sharing my feelings with you.
- I believe you will respect my boundaries.
- I can depend on you without needing to control you.
- When problems arise, we can communicate honestly.

**Trust says: "I feel safe placing confidence in you."**

Trust is not blind belief. It develops through experience and consistent actions.
